### About

I'm a **Java Developer at Tata Consultancy Services**, building scalable backend systems on **Spring Boot** — and over the past few months, I've been going deep into **Agentic AI** and **Retrieval-Augmented Generation**, designing multi-step reasoning pipelines with **LangGraph** and **LangChain**.

I like systems that *decide* before they act — adaptive routing, self-correcting agents, hybrid retrieval — rather than fixed, linear pipelines. That's the thread connecting my backend work and my AI work: clean architecture, sound decisions, production discipline.

- 🏢 Backend Engineer @ **TCS** — Java, Spring Boot, microservices
- 🤖 Building **Agentic AI / RAG** systems with LangGraph, LangChain, FAISS, Groq
- ☁️ Comfortable with **AWS** — EC2, S3, Lambda, IAM, and friends
- 🎓 B.E. in Computer Science Engineering, graduated
- 🧩 500+ DSA problems solved across LeetCode & GeeksforGeeks
